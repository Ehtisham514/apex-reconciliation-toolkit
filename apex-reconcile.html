CSV format & example files

Required columns: date,description,amount,account,currency. Optional columns: id,reference. Use YYYY-MM-DD dates, positive amounts for money in, and negative amounts for money out on both sides. Amounts support up to two decimal places. Quote amounts containing commas.

Give the same account the same label in both files. Accounts and currencies are never combined. Debit and credit exports must be converted to signed amounts first. Files must be UTF-8 CSV, no more than 3 MB each.

Suggested matches need your review. This tool compares the supplied transactions; it does not approve matches, post entries, or verify closing balances.
