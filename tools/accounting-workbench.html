CSV format & accounting assumptions

id,party,invoice_date,due_date,amount,paid,currency,entity

Use amounts and payments as nonnegative values with up to two decimals; paid cannot exceed amount. Paid must already reflect your selected as-of date. This file has no dated payment history. Future invoice dates are excluded from aging but retained in the detailed export. Credits, overpayments and foreign-exchange conversion need separate review. Due today belongs in Current.

Use UTF-8 CSV, up to 5,000 rows and 3 MiB. Required IDs must be unique. Entity labels are normalized for case and repeated whitespace in summary grouping.

CSV format & pairing rules

id,date,entity,counterparty,reference,amount,currency

Negative means money leaves entity; positive means it arrives. Names are normalized for case and repeated whitespace. References must match exactly after trimming, including punctuation and case. Blank references, repeated references within an entity pair/currency, zero values and ambiguous groups stay unresolved. No rows are removed.

Up to 5,000 rows and 3 MiB of UTF-8 CSV. Suggestions need evidence review; this tool does not create due-to/due-from entries or consolidation eliminations.
