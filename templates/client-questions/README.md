# Client questions for the monthly close

Use this pack when a payment, deposit or transfer appears in the books and you need the owner to explain it. The owner gets one short list. The accountant can trace each reply to the entry it supports.

[Download the client question sheet](https://github.com/Ehtisham514/apex-reconciliation-toolkit/raw/refs/heads/main/templates/client-questions/client-questions.csv) · [Download the accountant’s review log](https://github.com/Ehtisham514/apex-reconciliation-toolkit/raw/refs/heads/main/templates/client-questions/review-log.csv)

## For the accountant

1. Open the two blank CSVs in Excel or another spreadsheet. Make private working copies for one client, entity and close period. If your accounting software already has a request feature, the wording below also works there.
2. Assign each question a unique ID such as Q-01. Enter the date, statement description, amount, currency, and whether money came in or went out. Ask for one specific piece of information. Set a reply date you have agreed with the client.
3. Share the **client question sheet** using your usual private channel. Keep the **review log** with the working papers. Use a document reference that both parties recognize and keep the actual attachment in the agreed private location.
4. Start the review log at **Waiting for client**. When a reply arrives, mark it **Ready for review**. Record the evidence and what you did. Mark it **Closed** after review, or record the next question and return it to **Waiting for client**. If the books already reflect the transaction correctly, record the reason and leave the entry as it is.

## For the business owner

Fill in **Your answer** and, if you have a receipt or invoice, **Document reference**. A supplier name, invoice number, project name, or destination account nickname can be enough to locate the record. If you’re unsure, say what you know and who might have the rest.

## Three questions you can copy

All names, dates and amounts here are fictional.

| What the accountant sees | What the owner receives | What the accountant checks after the reply |
|---|---|---|
| Q-01 · 4 May · card payment · USD 86.40 | “What did you buy at North Street Supply for $86.40, and which job was it for? If you have the receipt, please add its reference.” | Match the receipt to the payment, check the job and confirm the expense account. |
| Q-02 · 7 May · customer deposit · USD 1,200 | “Who sent the $1,200 deposit on 7 May? An invoice number or payment reference would help me match it.” | Check the remittance and open invoices before applying the receipt. |
| Q-03 · 10 May · transfer out · USD 500 | “Which account received the $500 transfer on 10 May? The account nickname and the date it arrived are enough.” | Find the receiving entry and compare both sides before treating it as an internal transfer. |

## Message to send with the sheet

Hi [name], I’m finishing the books for [month] and need your help with [number] items. Could you fill in the answer column by [date]? I’ve included the date and amount so you can recognize each one. If you’re unsure about an item, just let me know. Thank you, [name].
