About the project

APEX Accounting & Solutions

Maintained by Ehtisham Ul Haq. This repository brings practical accounting workflows and transparent financial calculations into one organized collection.

Use the examples to explore, inspect the source code, and adapt the working papers to your own reporting process. Public contributions should use fictional data.

View source, tests and project history →

Understand the boundaries

This collection supports accounting and planning work. It does not provide live bank feeds, automatic journals, statutory filings, full ERP integration or enterprise service guarantees.

Financial forecasts and valuations depend on the inputs and assumptions. Trust, tax and regulatory requirements need the applicable professional review.
